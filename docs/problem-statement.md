# SlotSure Problem Statement

## The Problem
Small service businesses (salons, barbershops, clinics, tutors) rely on manual scheduling, pen-and-paper, or generic calendar apps. This leads to double-bookings, lost appointments, and no reliable way to manage staff availability.

## The Intended Payer
The business owner. SlotSure is a B2B SaaS tool—the owner pays a monthly subscription to manage their staff, services, and bookings.

## Why It Matters
A booking conflict isn't just a bug; it's a broken promise to a customer. Reliable booking software must prevent two customers from reserving the same staff member at the same time, even under concurrent requests.
